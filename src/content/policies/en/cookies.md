## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device to remember information about your visit.

This policy complies with Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the guidelines of the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

## 2. Cookies used by this site

Currently, **this website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies**, whether first-party or third-party.

The website uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to obtain aggregated information about visits and website performance. This service does not use cookies to track individual visitors.

Only **technical or necessary cookies** might be used if they are essential for the site to function, for example, to remember your language preference.

These cookies are exempt from the consent requirement under Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE.

For this reason, **no cookie consent banner is currently required** for the services used on this website.

## 3. Contact form

The contact form **does not install cookies**.

The processing of the data you provide through it is explained in the [Privacy Policy](/en/policies/privacy/).

## 4. Managing cookies

You can allow, block or delete cookies stored on your device through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge).

Disabling technical cookies may affect how the website works.

## 5. Changes to this policy

If the website introduces tools that use non-essential cookies, such as analytics, advertising or tracking technologies, this policy will be updated and an appropriate prior-consent mechanism will be implemented before those technologies are activated.
